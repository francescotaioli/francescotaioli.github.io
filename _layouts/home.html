Francesco Taioli

PhD student in Artificial Intelligence Member of IntelliGO Labs francesco.taioli@polito.it {%- include social.html -%}

I am a third-year PhD student in the National PhD Programme in Artificial Intelligence at the Polytechnic of Turin, in collaboration with the University of Verona. I am supervised by prof. Marco Cristani and prof. Alessandro Farinelli.

My research interests span the enitre area of Deep Learning, with a focus on enhancing the autonomy of intelligent agents. I am currently exploring Foundation Models and their use in Embodied AI to improve navigation, common sense reasoning, and human-robot interaction.

Research Interests

  • Deep Learning
  • Computer Vision
  • Embodied AI
  • Autonomous Vehicles
